PromptPouch Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data the PromptPouch Android app handles, where it is stored, and what choices you have.

Last updated: 17 October 2026 · Applies to PromptPouch (Android), version 1.0.0

PromptPouch is an offline-first workspace for building reusable prompts. It is designed so that your content stays on your device. The app has no user accounts, performs no analytics or tracking, and does not send your data to us or to any server.

1. Who we are

PromptPouch is developed and published by the developer of this app. The app is listed on Google Play under the package name com.morty01999.promptvault.

Developer privacy contact: rickch47@proton.me

2. Data the app stores (on your device only)

All content you create in PromptPouch is stored locally in the app's private database (promptvault.db, SQLite) in the app's sandboxed storage on your device. This can include:

  • Prompts and prompt versions you write.
  • Reusable context entries (facts, constraints, brand voice, and similar).
  • Memories (durable notes you choose to keep across prompts).
  • Projects and organizational tags.
  • Run history and assembled prompt outputs, and prompt packages.
  • App settings, including any instruction templates you customize.

This content is created by you. The app does not collect this data on our behalf, and no copy of it is transmitted to the developer.

3. Data the app does NOT collect

  • No account registration, login, or user profile.
  • No analytics, telemetry, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No collection of personal identifiers, location, contacts, or device identifiers by the app.
  • No cloud synchronization or remote storage of your content.

4. Network use and third-party AI services

The app itself makes no network requests and does not call any AI provider's API. Your prompt text is never sent anywhere by PromptPouch.

When you choose to use a prompt with an AI service, the app:

  1. Copies the assembled prompt text to your device clipboard, and
  2. Opens the third-party service you selected (for example ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, or Perplexity), either in its installed app or in your web browser.

You then paste the text yourself. PromptPouch does not transmit the prompt or attach it to any URL. Once you paste your content into a third-party AI service, that service's own privacy policy and terms apply, and the developer of PromptPouch has no control over and no visibility into how that service handles your content. Review those policies before sharing sensitive information.

5. Clipboard, sharing, and import/export

  • Clipboard: used only when you tap a copy action. The app writes the text you asked to copy. It does not read your clipboard.
  • Backup & export: when you export a backup, the app creates a JSON file in its own cache directory and hands it to the Android share sheet, so you can save or send it wherever you choose. This only happens when you initiate it.
  • Import: when you import a backup, the app reads only the file you pick with the system file picker, validates it, and writes it into the local database.

Because export produces a plain file with your content, store and share backups carefully.

6. Device permissions

  • Internet — declared by the app framework. The app performs no network requests of its own.
  • Vibrate — used for optional haptic feedback.
  • Read/write external storage — requested only on Android 12 and below, to support saving/reading backup files.
  • Display over other apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW) — not requested. This permission is explicitly removed from the app manifest.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • Your content remains on your device until you delete it within the app or remove the app.
  • Deleting individual items removes them from the local database.
  • Uninstalling PromptPouch removes its app-private data from the device.
  • Because the app keeps no server-side copy, there is nothing for the developer to delete on your behalf.

The app allows Android's system backup (allowBackup is enabled). Depending on your device settings, Android may include app data in a device backup associated with your Google account. You can control device backup in your Android system settings.

8. Security

Your data is kept in the app's private storage area, which the operating system isolates from other apps. The app does not currently apply additional encryption to the local database beyond the protections provided by the operating system.

9. Children's privacy

PromptPouch is a productivity tool for people who build and use prompts with large language model (LLM) apps. It is not directed to children, and it is not designed for or marketed to children. The app has no accounts and no network features, and it does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

10. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change (for example, if any online feature, account, analytics, or purchase is added), this policy will be updated before that change takes effect, and the "Last updated" date above will be revised.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, contact: rickch47@proton.me.
